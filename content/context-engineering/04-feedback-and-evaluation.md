# Feedback and evaluation

## Why it matters
AI quality improves when the workflow includes evaluation. Small feedback loops tell you whether the model is solving the actual problem or just producing plausible text.

## What to evaluate
- Correctness against the real task
- Relevance of the gathered context
- Clarity and completeness of the output
- Safety of the final action

## Real project example
A teammate tests the generated change by checking whether it really fixed the issue, not whether it sounded polished. That feedback loop quickly surfaces weak outputs before they get merged.

> **Pro tip:** judge outputs by the result, not the confidence of the phrasing.
